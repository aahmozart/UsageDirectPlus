UsageDirectPlus tracks your daily screen time and keeps a permanent log of how long you used each app, and when.

It is a fork of usageDirect that adds:

– Usage intervals: the start and end time of every app session, not just a daily total
– Usage timeline and unlock timeline charts for each day
– Screen on/off and lock/unlock event history
– Database export to a folder of your choice, on demand or on a schedule, with optional GZIP compression

Like the original, it also offers:

– Reliable app usage statistics from up to ten days before the app was installed
– Historical data in charts
– Usage times on a clock pie chart per day
– Weekday average chart
– Daily usage time in a widget

All data is stored in a database on your device. The app has no internet permission; nothing leaves your device unless you export or share the database yourself.

The app needs the usage access permission to read usage statistics, and lists installed apps in order to show their names and icons.
